bulb

A money assistant for your household, from LightHouse Wealth Partners.

bulb finds the money matters in your accounts and your email (subscriptions you no longer use, refunds still owed, bills and deadlines) and takes care of them with your OK, by text message.

What bulb does

  • Finds subscriptions and repeat charges, and cancels the ones you don't want after you reply "ok".
  • Follows refunds, claims and credits until the money is back.
  • Reminds you before deadlines: dispute windows, renewals, tax dates.
  • Answers questions about your household's money by iMessage.

How bulb uses your Gmail

  • With your permission, bulb reads your email (read-only) to find receipts, bills, renewal notices, refunds and other money emails. It ignores personal email.
  • When you ask, bulb writes an email for you, such as a cancellation request, and sends it only after you reply "ok" to that exact email.
  • Your data is kept in an encrypted database on your own computer.
  • You can disconnect Gmail or delete everything at any time by texting bulb "delete my data".
